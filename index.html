Is this the right circle for you?

Perfect for you if

  • Your baby is between 6 and 18 months.
  • You want a weekly rhythm with other grown-ups and their little ones.
  • You would rather sing along, in tune or not, than watch from the side.
  • You want something that is for your baby and restores you too.

Maybe not yet if

  • Your baby is under 6 months. The songs and movement are built for sitting, reaching, and early crawling.
  • You are looking for a drop-off class. Grown-ups stay and take part.
  • You want formal music instruction. This is play with a purpose, not lessons.
